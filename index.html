What we do

Two trades under one roof. Most of our customers call about one and end up asking about the other, because the wall and the roof usually fail together.

Tuckpointing

Grinding out failed mortar joints and repacking them so the wall sheds water again.

Brick & stone repair

Replacing spalled and cracked brick, rebuilding sections of wall, matching what is there.

Chimney repair & new chimneys

Rebuilding crowns and stacks, and building chimneys new from the roofline up.

Lintel replacement

Cutting out rusted steel lintels above windows and doors before they push the brick apart.

Parapet walls

The wall above the roofline that nobody looks at until water is coming through the ceiling.

Masonry waterproofing

Sealing brick and stone once the joints are sound, so the repair lasts.

Concrete & flatwork

Driveways, sidewalks, porches, stairs and slabs, including cutting and removal.

Roofing

Tear-offs, new decking, underlayment and shingles, plus flat roofs.

Commercial work

Flat commercial roofs and commercial masonry and concrete.

Recent jobs

Full roof replacement in Harvey

We tore the old roof off down to the frame, found the decking was past saving, and replaced it with new plywood. Underlayment went down over that, then shingles. A tear-off is the only way to know what the decking underneath is actually doing — shingling over a soft deck buys a homeowner about two years.

A house tuckpointed top to bottom

Not a patch on the worst wall — every elevation, ground to roofline, ground out and repacked. When mortar has gone that far the piecemeal approach just moves the leak to the next joint, so we did the whole envelope at once.

Decking, paint and brick flower beds

One homeowner had us replace the deck on the back of the house, paint it, and then build brick flower beds around the property. Different trades in one visit, one crew, one invoice — which is usually why people keep our number.
