Water is almost always the answer

Nearly every masonry repair we are called out for traces back to water and to the freeze-thaw cycle that follows it. Water gets into a wall through a failed joint, a cracked crown, a rusting lintel or an open coping seam. Then it freezes, expands, opens the gap it came in through, and lets more water in next time.

Which is why we will always want to find the entry point rather than just tidy up the damage. Fix the symptom and you are booking the same job again in three years.

The work, by type

Tuckpointing

Grinding out dead mortar and repacking the joints. The most common job on this list by a distance.

Brick & stone repair

Spalled faces, cracked courses and rebuilt sections, matched to the existing brick.

Chimneys

Crowns, repointing, partial and full rebuilds — and new chimneys built from scratch.

Lintels

Rusted steel above windows and doors, cut out and replaced before it splits the wall.

Parapet walls

The wall above the roofline, and the coping across the top of it.

Waterproofing

Sealing sound masonry to protect the repair — after the joints are right, not instead.

Concrete

Driveways, walks, porches, steps and slabs, plus cutting and removal.

Commercial masonry

Larger buildings, scheduled around your tenants and your trading hours.

Fitting the work to the budget

There is nearly always more than one honest answer. A wall might want full repointing, or it might want the two elevations that take the weather done now and the others in a couple of years. A chimney might want rebuilding, or it might want the crown recast to buy real time.

We will lay out the options and tell you what each one actually buys you. We can fit within most budgets, and after forty-five years we have no interest in selling anybody a job they did not need.
